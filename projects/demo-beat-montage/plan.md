# Plan: Cold Steel (beat-sync demo)

Status: APPROVED (Phase 13 acceptance demo 1, BUILD_PLAN §5). Recipe: beat-sync. Format: reels 1080×1920, 30 fps. Grade: bw_contrast.

## Concept
An 18 s black-and-white action montage that hits every beat: a quiet build of city, airship and sniper shots, a moment of silence, then the drop slams in with a flash and a punch, and the cuts land on every beat until a slow-motion ending.

## Structure and timings
| # | Time | Content | Music (Go Cart from 1:08) | Cut anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–3.0 | City skyline, slow push-in, "COLD STEEL" slams in | low section, no kicks | start |
| 2 | 3.0–5.1 | Airship (glitch shader in) | downbeat | downbeat 2.98 |
| 3 | 5.1–7.1 | Sniper aims: 1× → 0.25× RIFE slow motion → 1× | downbeat | downbeat 5.07 |
| 4 | 7.1–8.2 | Rifle close-up, push-in; music ducks to silence 7.75–8.17 | riser | downbeat 7.14 |
| 5 | 8.2 | **Drop**: eye close-up, white flash, zoom punch + shake, boom, "NO FEAR" | drop 76.196 s | drop (first kick) |
| 6–11 | 9.2–15.5 | Cathedral, walk, tower, old man ×2, two people: a cut every 2 beats, punches on beats, chromatic-split shader at 14.5 | high section | downbeats/beats |
| 12 | 15.5–18.0 | Sniper, flash + impact, slows to 0.3× (RIFE), "STEEL." pops | outro, fade 1.2 s | downbeat 15.52 |

## Music
"Go Cart – Drop Mix", Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0), 115 BPM, beat grid reliable; used from 1:08 so the drop (1:16.196) lands at 8.2 s.

## Shots
All from the Tears of Steel excerpt (Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0), shots chosen from `analysis/32c187bc_tos-60-180/sheets/contact.jpg` by motion/faces; reframed 16:9 → 9:16 with fixed focus points.

## On-screen text
COLD STEEL (Anton, slam) · NO FEAR (drop) · STEEL. (end). English only (demo).

## Transitions and effects
2 shader transitions (glitch, chromatic-split), 2 flash cuts, zoom punches + shake on the drop, 2 RIFE speed ramps, B&W LUT. SFX: swish, riser (ends on the drop), boom, impact (own synthesised).
